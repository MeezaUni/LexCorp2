# Brag Plan: LexCorp

## What is this app?
An air-gapped, zero-trust enterprise security platform combining W3C DIDs, ERC-721 On-Chain Digital Asset Twins, and Embedded Local AI Anomaly Detection to secure defense assets, supply chain provenance, and mission-critical access control.

## The angle
High-tech defense enterprise launch. Serious, precise, and impressive. An end-to-end look at how high-security physical and digital assets are minted on an immutable blockchain ledger, protected with non-custodial crypto, and audited by a local air-gapped AI risk engine.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on a dark modern background: "DEFENSE-GRADE ASSET MANAGEMENT. RE-ENGINEERED." with a subtle glowing pulse, followed by an immediate reveal of the LexCorp dashboard.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Asset Minting**: Simulation of the Manager Dashboard entering a serial like `BEL-RADAR-2026-001`, signing non-custodially, and generating a tamper-evident cryptographic QR code.
- **On-Chain Immutability**: The transaction being registered onto a zero-gas Hyperledger Besu consortium ledger with zero token speculation.
- **Air-Gapped AI Risk Engine**: The Auditor Dashboard picking up the event via the indexer, and the local `IsolationForest` model scoring it with an explicit "LOW RISK" badge.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero Trust. Zero Gas. Fully Air-Gapped."
Logo lockup: **LexCorp** — Enterprise Blockchain Identity & Asset Management.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry & Mint**: Manager types `BEL-RADAR-2026-001`, clicks "Mint Digital Twin".
2. **Blockchain Confirm**: Transaction hashes and registers on-chain.
3. **Auditor & AI Detection**: Event streams to the Auditor Dashboard with an AI Risk score attached.
